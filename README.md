# Healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) Analytics Dashboard
Healthcare Claims Analytics Dashboard using Power BI, DAX, Power Query, and Excel.


## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing healthcare Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) data using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into claim submissions, denials, payer performance, authorization exceptions, and denial recovery opportunities.

The objective is to help healthcare finance teams, RCM managers, and operational stakeholders monitor claim performance, identify revenue leakage, and make data-driven decisions to improve revenue recovery.

## Business Objectives

- Monitor healthcare claim submission and denial performance.
- Identify major denial reasons and payer-related issues.
- Analyze authorization exceptions and compliance gaps.
- Identify potential appeal candidates and recovery opportunities.
- Support better decision-making through interactive dashboards.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization.
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX** – Calculated measures and business KPIs.
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset preparation and data source.

## Dashboard Reports

### Report 1 – Claims Overview
- Total Claims Submitted
- Claim Status Analysis

- Overall Claims Performance

### Report 2 – Denial Analysis
- Denial Rate Analysis

- Denial Reasons
- Payer-wise Denial Analysis
- CPT-wise Denial Insights

### Report 3 – Authorization & Denial Recovery
- Potential Authorization Exceptions
- Claims for Manual Review
- Potential Appeal Candidates
- Estimated Recoverable Amount (where supported by the dataset)
- Recovery Action Breakdown
- Recovery Estimate by Denial Category
- Appeal Success Probability Bands
- Authorization Exception Analysis

### Report 4 – Claim-Level Drill-Through
- Detailed claim information
- Claim status and denial reason
- Payer and CPT details
- Authorization information
- Recovery and appeal-related details

## Key Business Insights

- Identifies claim denial patterns and potential revenue leakage.
- Highlights payer-related authorization exceptions.
- Helps prioritize claims for manual review.
- Identifies potential appeal and recovery opportunities.
- Provides claim-level details for further investigation.
